## Hashtag sets — reference

Real terms used in the French parkour community, mixed with hyper-local geography.
Verified current, not pulled from memory alone: *traceur* is the standard French term for
a parkour practitioner, *freerunning* and *art du déplacement* are the two adjacent
disciplines practitioners actually distinguish between (see `docs/brand/narrative.md`'s
vocabulary section for why loose synonyms like "urban movement" don't belong in this
brand's mouth). Avoid generic fitness tags entirely — `#motivation`, `#fitness`,
`#instagood` and the like signal nothing to a parkour audience and dilute reach toward
people who will never care.

**Core (use on almost everything):**
`#parkour #parkourfrance #bassindarcachon #gironde`

**Community / credibility posts:**
`#traceur #freerunning #artdudeplacement #parkourlife #parkourtraining`

**Hyper-local (spot check-ins, club-adjacent posts):**
`#gujanmestras #latestedebuch #bordeaux #arcachon`

**Branded, optional:** `#vertiflow` is fine as a light branded tag for archive/repost
tracking. Do not invent a campaign hashtag and present it as if the community already uses
it — if a new one is wanted (something like `#OnCommenceIci`, echoing the door
positioning), it should be introduced deliberately as a new tag Maxime is seeding, not
implied to have any existing usage.

Keep total tags around 8–12: 3–4 from Core, 2–3 from Community or Local depending on the
post, one branded tag at most. Place them at the end of the caption itself rather than the
first comment — keeps the full post intact on save and share, and the old "hide it in the
first comment" habit doesn't buy what it used to.
