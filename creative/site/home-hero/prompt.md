## Prompt

Ultra-photorealistic editorial night photograph of a single athletic man standing on a low
concrete ledge in an empty urban plaza in a French coastal town, immediately after rain.

The man is the person in the supplied reference photographs. Preserve his face, build and
proportions faithfully. Late twenties, lean and athletic, calm and still — not mid-movement,
not posing. He stands three-quarters to camera, weight on one leg, looking off-frame right.
He wears the black VertiFlow CLIMB t-shirt from the product references, dark technical
trousers, understated low-profile trainers.

Environment: weathered concrete ledges, a scuffed steel handrail, wide shallow steps, an
asphalt forecourt holding standing water with long amber reflections. Ordinary French
municipal architecture in the far background, unlit and out of focus.

Light: one sodium-vapour street lamp out of frame to the left, warm amber raking across
wet concrete and rim-lighting his shoulder to separate the black shirt from the dark
background. The upper third of the frame is deep unlit black. A single distant window
provides restrained cool cyan as the only counter-colour.

Full-frame camera, 35mm lens, f/2.8, tripod, long exposure. Realistic high-ISO grain, true
light falloff, imperfect reflections, real fabric folds and weave, visible pores and
flyaway hair, moss in the concrete joints.

Composition: he sits in the right third of the frame. The upper-left third stays quiet,
dark and uncluttered for overlaid typography. Horizon low. Generous negative space above.

He is alone. No other people, no silhouettes, no figures in the background. This is a
brand campaign photograph, not a training session.
