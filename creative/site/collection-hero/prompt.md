**References:** `creative/references/maxime/` + the product images for this drop

Regenerated for every collection. The environment stays constant so drops read as a series;
only the garment changes.

## Prompt

Ultra-photorealistic editorial night photograph of a single athletic man seated on the edge
of a wide concrete step in an empty urban plaza in a French coastal town, after rain.

The man is the person in the supplied reference photographs. Preserve his face, build and
proportions faithfully. He is seated, forearms on knees, relaxed and still, looking down
and away from camera. He wears [GARMENT] from the product references — preserve its fabric
colour, the small circular white VertiFlow chest mark, and the printed graphic exactly as
supplied, undistorted, printed into the weave rather than pasted on.

Environment: broad wet concrete steps, a steel handrail running out of frame, standing
water below holding long amber reflections. Unlit municipal architecture far behind, out of
focus.

Light: one sodium-vapour street lamp out of frame to the right, warm amber across the wet
steps and rim-lighting his shoulders and jaw. Deep unlit black across the top of the frame.
A single restrained cyan reflection in the water as the only counter-colour.

Full-frame camera, 35mm lens, f/2.8, tripod, long exposure. Realistic high-ISO grain, true
falloff, real textile folds and weave, natural skin texture.

Composition: he sits centre-right. The lower-left is quiet and dark for the collection name
in overlaid typography. Camera at seated eye level.

He is alone. No other people. Brand campaign photograph, not a training session.

## Negative

teal-orange grading, lens flares, vignetting, fake readable signage, warped or mirrored
garment graphics, duplicated limbs, malformed hands, extra fingers, watermarks, borders,
floating typography, crowds, action poses.
