# Slot: home-story

**Output:** 2000×1500 (4:3) · **Quiet region:** none — this one is full-bleed texture
**References:** none
**Inherits:** `../../house-look.md`

## Prompt

Ultra-photorealistic macro detail photograph, at night, of the worn top edge of a steel
handrail in a public square after rain.

The steel is scuffed to bare metal along the top from years of hands and feet, with paint
surviving only on the underside and in the pitting. Beads of water sit along the edge. Fine
scratches catch the light.

This is a photograph about wear — about a thing that has been used for a long time by a lot
of people. Nothing pristine, nothing staged.

Light: sodium-vapour street lighting from the upper left, warm amber picking out the
polished metal edge against a deep black out-of-focus background. Extremely shallow depth
of field.

Full-frame camera, 100mm macro lens, f/2.8, tripod. Realistic high-ISO grain, true
specular highlights on wet metal, visible texture in the paint chips and rust pitting.

Composition: the rail runs diagonally across the frame. Background falls away into black
bokeh with a single soft amber highlight.

No people, no hands, no chalk dust, no branding.

## Negative

AI gloss, HDR halos, oversaturation, cyberpunk neon, purple grading, lens flares,
vignetting, watermarks, borders, people, hands, text, logos, pristine or new-looking metal,
studio lighting.
