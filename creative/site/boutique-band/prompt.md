# Slot: boutique-band

**Output:** 2400×1000 (12:5) · **Quiet region:** centre, for the section title
**References:** none
**Inherits:** `../../house-look.md`

A wide atmospheric band sitting above the permanent range. Establishes place; sells nothing.

## Prompt

Ultra-photorealistic wide night photograph of an empty coastal-town square after rain,
shot straight on at low camera height.

Flat wet paving fills the lower half, holding long unbroken amber reflections. Low concrete
ledges and a steel rail sit in the middle distance. Beyond them, unlit shopfronts and pine
trees typical of the Bassin d'Arcachon, deep in shadow and out of focus.

Light: sodium-vapour street lamps out of frame on both sides, warm amber pooling in the wet
paving and falling off fast into black. The upper third is near-black sky. One distant cool
cyan window as the only counter-colour.

Full-frame camera, 35mm lens, f/4, tripod, long exposure. Realistic high-ISO grain, true
reflections with slight ripple, real weathering in the paving joints.

Composition: strictly symmetrical, horizon low, deep perspective running to a vanishing
point at centre. The centre of the frame stays quiet and dark for an overlaid section
title. Extreme letterbox crop — the composition must survive at 12:5.

No people, no vehicles, no legible signage.

## Negative

AI gloss, HDR halos, oversaturation, cyberpunk neon, purple or teal-orange grading, lens
flares, vignetting, fake readable signage, watermarks, borders, people, cars, bicycles,
busy detail near the centre.
