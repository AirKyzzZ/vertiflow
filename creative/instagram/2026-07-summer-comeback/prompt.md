# VertiFlow Summer Comeback Carousel

## Generation method

Built-in OpenAI image generation using the existing CLIMB front, back, and full-garment product images as references.

## Prompt

Use case: ads-marketing

Asset type: one seamless three-slide Instagram carousel panorama, later divided into three adjacent 4:5 portrait slides.

Create an exceptionally photorealistic nighttime fashion photograph in downtown Bangkok featuring a young athletic parkour athlete wearing the exact black VertiFlow CLIMB T-shirt shown in the reference images, standing safely beside a stationary urban train or at a public railway-platform environment. The image must feel like a real high-end streetwear campaign shot on location, never like CGI or an AI render.

Preserve the garment's black fabric, small circular white VertiFlow chest mark, and large CLIMB typography with iridescent carabiner graphic on the back as faithfully as possible.

The scene is authentic Bangkok at night, with an elevated rail or railway setting in a safe public pedestrian area, hints of Thai urban architecture, warm train-window glow, cyan and magenta city reflections, humid air, subtly wet pavement, and real commuters in soft distant motion blur.

Show one believable young adult parkour athlete with a lean athletic build, natural skin and anatomy, viewed mainly from behind in a relaxed three-quarter stance so the CLIMB back design is clearly visible and not distorted. The athlete is stationary and safely separated from the train tracks, wearing modern dark trousers and understated sneakers.

Use ultra-realistic editorial streetwear photography, a full-frame camera and 35mm lens, natural depth of field, realistic high-ISO film grain, real textile folds, pores, flyaway hair, imperfect city reflections, and practical lighting.

Create an extremely wide 12:5 master panorama designed to be cropped into exactly three seamless equal 4:5 portrait panels. The train and platform lines continue continuously across all three panels. Panel 1 carries Bangkok and train atmosphere. Panel 2 contains the full athlete and complete shirt design, with no important garment detail crossing a panel boundary. Panel 3 continues the train into city depth and movement energy. All panels have visual interest and align seamlessly at both crop boundaries.

Use cinematic but plausible available night light, moody comeback energy, a rim light separating black fabric from the background, and a clearly visible shirt graphic that does not look pasted on. Keep the palette deep black, charcoal, muted steel, Bangkok amber light, and restrained cyan and magenta reflections.

Do not add captions, slogans, logos, watermarks, fake UI, or floating typography. Only preserve the existing printed shirt artwork. Avoid AI gloss, plastic skin, oversharpening, excessive neon, cyberpunk fantasy, impossible train tracks, warped letters, duplicated people, malformed anatomy, fake readable signage, borders, triptych dividers, or visible crop lines.

## Export

- Master: 3240 x 1350 PNG
- Slides: three 1080 x 1350 JPEG files, uploaded in numeric order
